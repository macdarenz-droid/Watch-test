# PH2 paper tag: what it is, how it is logged, and its PROMOTION RULE - written Fri 2015-05-01 07:00 London, before its first paper day
Coach, 05-01: "on PH2 as a 2nd paper tag: yes, same rule as UN3 - write the paper call before each live day, write the promotion rule now, and it never touches
your live rules."

## What PH2 is
When the hour before my entry (07:00-08:00 London, twelve M5 candles) has a range of 30 pips or more ("lively"), the paper version takes the A2 trade at HALF size.
Otherwise it is A2. Build result (journal/2015-idea-PH-pre-entry-hour.md): 30 lively mornings in 82 earned +0.146R against +0.572R for the quiet ones, and carried 9 of
14 full-stop mornings; the explanation missed its third line (46% < 50%) and PH2 FAILED the profit line (K1). It is not a survivor. It is an observation with a tally.

## What it may and may not do
- LIVE RULE: A2 at 0.50%, every weekday, unchanged. A lively pre-entry hour is traded live at FULL size. Halving, skipping or delaying a live trade because of PH2 is a
  TRADE MISTAKE (wrong size / skipped day), whatever the result. The only live size triggers are the two written on 04-27.
- PAPER ONLY: one line in the daily tally. No money, no effect on triggers or payouts.

## How it is logged
- PH2 cannot be known at 07:00: the hour it measures ends at 08:00. So its call has two parts, both before the order: in the 07:00 call the line
  "PH2: decided at 08:00"; in the 08:00 note, BEFORE the order is typed: "PH2: pre-entry hour high x, low y = z pips -> LIVELY (paper half size) / quiet (paper full size)".
  The range is read from the tool (the four M15 candles 07:00-08:00 London; highest high minus lowest low), not estimated. A day without that line does not count for
  the tag and is a process mistake.
- Forward counting starts today, Fri 2015-05-01. The 82 build days never count (including Thursday 04-30, which I counted by hand before the tag existed).
- Paper result: lively day = half of the live A2 result as traded; quiet day = the live result. Tally: forward days, lively days, A2's R and full-stop mornings on
  lively and on quiet days, PH2 net R and max drawdown beside A2's.
- Frozen: 07:00-08:00 London, 30 pips, half size. No second threshold, no "except when".

## PROMOTION RULE (decided now)
No look at promotion before BOTH: (a) 200 forward trading days since 05-01, AND (b) at least 60 forward lively days. (At the recent rate that is about ten months.)
Then PH2 may be PROPOSED to my coach - not adopted - only if ALL hold on the forward days alone:
  P1. A2's average R on lively days is at least 0.30R below its average on quiet days, and that gap is at least two standard errors;
  P2. the full-stop-morning rate (40+ pips against the entry before noon) on lively days is at least twice the rate on quiet days;
  P3. the gap in P1 is still at least 0.15R after removing the three worst lively days;
  P4. the gap is positive in at least 3 of the 4 consecutive quarters of the forward period;
  P5. the coach's line, "don't filter winners away": PH2's net R is at least 90% of A2's over the same days AND its maximum drawdown is at most 80% of A2's
      (it gives up profit, so it must buy a real reduction in drawdown);
  P6. lively days are between 20% and 60% of the forward days (if nearly every morning is "lively", this is just "trade half size", which is a size decision, not a filter).
If all six hold: a one-page proposal with the forward table; my coach decides; a change to the live rule needs the coach's written yes.
A few good paper days, a losing week, or "it would have halved today's stop" are not reasons.

## KILL RULE (decided now)
- At the first look: if P1 or P5 fails, the tag is CLOSED and the idea is dead. No extension.
- Early close: after the first 40 forward lively days, if A2's average on them is not below its average on the quiet days of the same period, closed then.
- If I change a live trade because of it: closed that day, and the trade is tagged a mistake.
