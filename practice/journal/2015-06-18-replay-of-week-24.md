## Does a mistake repeat?
1. MY mistakes (a line of the plan broken): none on any of the six days. Calls committed before the bell, full size, stop never moved, noon exit, one trade.
   Checked again against the journal: six of six. (The 06-10 review of the last ten losses said the same for four of these days.)
2. A pattern in what the better trader saw, three times (Mon, Tue, Thu): THE LAST MOVE BEFORE THE BELL WAS A SHARP ONE, AND I ENTERED RIGHT AFTER IT ON A
   SIDE THE DAY THEN REFUSED. From my 08:00 notes: all four losing entries of the week were "better than at 07:00" - the hour before the bell had moved
   against my side (9.5, 43.1, 10.3 and 4.2 pips); the one winner was 21.5 pips "worse" - the hour had moved my way.
   Before I call that a mistake that repeats, the twins, from the same notes one week later:
   | day | pre-entry hour against my side by | result |
   |---|---|---|
   | Mon 06-15 | 35.6 pips | +0.23R |
   | Tue 06-16 | 35.6 pips (a 40 pip vertical candle against me at the bell - Monday 06-08's picture exactly) | +1.60R |
   | Wed 06-17 | 11.3 pips | +0.14R |
   | Thu 06-18 | 7.6 pips | +1.20R |
   Four of four lost in W24; four of four won in W25. Same picture at the bell, opposite weeks. And it is not a new question: the direction and size of
   the hour before entry were looked at in ET, LD, PH, BH, LM and in BS (hypothesis A) three days ago; "do not sell into a spike" is idea H (no chase), killed.
   This is the eighth time I see it: the losers I look at have winning twins - this time the twins arrived the very next week.
3. A second pattern, once: Thursday's third buy at the same price under falling highs. One case. "Third entry at the same price after two failures" has
   happened once in 117 days that I know of. Not testable, and "a rule made from one replayed day" is on my ruled-out list.
4. Wednesday's give-back: already a written forward question (GB1). Not re-opened.

## Verdict
No mistake repeats. As the coach wrote, no test. Nothing changes.
What the better trader had and I do not: on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday he would have been right to stand aside, and on Tuesday 06-16 he would have
stood aside from +1.60R for the same reason. I cannot tell his good reading from his bad one at 08:00, and 85 ideas say I cannot learn to from this data.
What I keep from the week, for myself and not as a rule:
- The week cost 2.29R and every day of it was traded as written. That is the result I can control, and it was 5 of 5.
- The noon exit took me out before Friday 06-05's 170 pip candle and before Wednesday 06-17's 110 pip evening. In a week when I looked hard at what
  the rule does badly, the two largest moves on the charts were ones it kept me out of.
- My worry line was right on Thursday 06-11 and wrong on Tuesday 06-16. It stays a line in the call and never becomes an order.
