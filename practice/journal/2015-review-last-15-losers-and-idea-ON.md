## Before I believe it: why this review is a biased way to find a rule
- I looked at LOSERS ONLY. Anything common on my mornings will be "shared" by them. Base rate, counted on all 78 signal days without looking at results:
  the night (00:00 -> 08:00 London) ended against my side on 37 days (47%); it did so in a one-way manner (net move at least half of the night's whole range) on 20 days (26%):
  3 in January, 5 in February, 12 in March-to-now. So "the night was against me" describes nearly half of ALL my mornings.
- I have tested the nearest thing before: idea NM (03-31), "skip when the night moved against my side" - on JANUARY ONLY, and it was backwards (the skipped trades were winners).
  The night study on 04-22 added: nights normally go against the previous day's move. So a night against me is the ordinary case, and in January it was the good case.
- All 15 reviewed losers lie between 02-27 and 04-22. A rule drawn from them will fit that stretch by construction. The only part of my history this review did not
  touch is 01-06 .. 02-26 (38 trades) - and for January I already know NM's answer. I expect the lesson to fail there. That is what the test is for.

## Hypothesis
When the night has moved one way against my side by the time I enter (00:00 -> 08:00 London: net move against me, at least half of the night's range), London tends to
continue it, and the A2 trade has clearly lower expectancy - in the months I did not review as well as in the ones I did.
