## Hypothesis
When the price trades clearly beyond yesterday's high (or low) and then closes back inside the range, the break has failed and the
price travels back into the range.

## Rule (London time, signals 08:00-16:00, one position at a time, each side at most once a day)
Yesterday's range = the previous London day (22:00 -> 22:00) high and low.
Break = an M15 candle whose high is 10+ pips above yesterday's high (or low 10+ pips below yesterday's low).
Failure = within the next 8 M15 candles (2 hours) a candle CLOSES back inside yesterday's range.
- FB1 (judged): at the next candle's open, market order back into the range (sell after a failed high break), stop 30 pips, out 3 hours after entry.
- FB2 (dose): the break went 20+ pips beyond.
- FB0 (control): the trade in the break's direction at the same moment.
Three variants, no more. Costs on (1.0 + 0.1); stressed 2.0 + 0.5. $125 = 1R.
