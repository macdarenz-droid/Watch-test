## Judgement, by what the period can and cannot say
- The 20 days did not test the choice. Neither the old 5% line nor the new 7% line was touched; under either rule I would have traded exactly the same
  20 trades at the same size. So the lived record is silent on "5% or 7%" - and I say that instead of reading a verdict into a losing month.
- It was nevertheless the right kind of period to hold a rule through: 11 full stops in 20 trades, five losses in a row at the end, a new deepest
  drawdown. I did not touch the size, the stop or the line. That part of the test - can I leave it alone - is passed.
- What I have besides the 20 days is the simulation record, unchanged: WS (7% at 0.50% reaches my own stop in 1.6% of two-year runs at the planning
  edge against 19.7% for 5%, never within 2% of the firm's line) and GD (with a dead edge the 7% guard stops me in about 70% of two-year runs at a cost of
  about 5%; with a small real edge it lets me collect a few hundred trades). The coach's decision of 06-02 rests on exactly that guard.
- The cost I named on 05-15 is now closer than it was: "if my edge is gone, I find out 2% later." With the lived average at +0.17R +-0.25R, that sentence
  is no longer abstract. It is still the trade-off I chose, with the reasons written before the trade of 05-15.
## Decision
KEEP: stop trading at 7% under the high-water ($23,510.81 today), half size from 3% under ($24,522.03), size 0.50%. No change proposed.
The half-size step is the next line that can act - possibly tomorrow - and it acts by the rule, on the day's close.
Next look at this rule: at each month-end review, with the lowest balance of the month against all three lines; and immediately if the half-size
trigger and then a further 2% are ever used up.
