# TRADING PLAN v2 - FUNDED - ONE PAGE (rewritten Wed 2015-06-17 after funded day 59; coach's brick)
The long version with every "why" and date is journal/PLAN-v2-funded-long-history-to-2015-06-16.md. Nothing in the rules changed in this rewrite.
Account: FundedNext funded $25,000. Firm's lines: 5% daily loss, 10% max loss ($22,500), 3% max risk open, 80% split, payout every 14 days.
Goal: steady monthly profit, safe payouts, never a breach.

## 1. The trade (rule A2, frozen since 2015-01-09; all times London)
- EURUSD only. One trade every weekday. No skips. No second trade.
- 07:00: look at D1 / H4 / H1 / M15, write the call (bias, levels, plan, risk) and commit it before the entry bar.
- Direction: the last 22:00 close against the 22:00 close three trading days earlier. Lower -> SELL, higher -> BUY. Any gap counts.
- 08:00: market order. Stop 40 pips from the bid at 08:00. Never moved. No target, no partial, no break-even, no trail.
- 12:00: close at market. Flat every afternoon, night and weekend.

## 2. Size rule (the real safety rule)
- Full size: 0.50% of $25,000 = $125 = 0.31 lots. Not raised after wins, not raised to win a loss back, not cut on a feeling.
- HALF size (0.16 lots) from a day's CLOSE at or below 3% under the high-water mark, until a new high-water.
- STOP TRADING from a day's close at or below 7% under the high-water mark: written review, restart only with my coach.
- The high-water moves up with new highs and down only by a payout. After the 06-19 payout: high-water $25,256.94; half size at $24,499.23; stop at $23,488.95.
- Size changes ONLY by a trigger written in advance. None is written. Sizing test 06-17 (idea SG): no size breaches (the 7% stop sees to it); at the planning
  edge 0.75% pays 28% more and meets my own stop in 22% of two-year runs against 3%; at +0.10R it pays nothing more. 0.50% stays.

## 3. Daily stop
- One trade, one stop: the planned worst day is -1R = -$125 = -0.5% (the firm allows 5%). Seen worst with a jump through the stop: about 3R = 1.5%.
- After the trade is closed - win, loss or stop - the day is over. No re-entry. No afternoon trade.

## 4. Payout rule (every 14 days from 03-27; next Fri 07-03)
- On the date, after the day's trade, on the tracker balance (sim balance minus everything paid out):
  below $26,000 -> request HALF of what is above $25,000 (half-cents rounded down); at or above $26,000 -> request everything above $26,000.
- No request while half size or the stop is on. The high-water is lowered by the amount requested. I keep 80%.
- Applied exactly as written, no exceptions either way. So far: $567.04 requested on 6 dates, $453.63 mine.

## 5. What I never do
- Never trade without the committed call. Never skip a weekday. Never move, widen or remove the stop. Never close before 12:00 by choice. Never hold past 12:00.
- Never add, average, re-enter or take a second trade. Never change the size except by rule 2.
- Never change a rule because of a losing day, week or month, or because of a big win. Never change a rule from fewer than a few hundred lived trades
  unless the idea has beaten A2 on history AND on lived forward days, with its kill lines written first, and the coach has agreed.
- Never let a paper tag (UN3, PH2, MB1, the +80 trail count) touch a live trade.
- Never write a number that I did not read from the tool. Never open the feed directly; never look past the released clock.

## 6. What a mistake is
- TRADE MISTAKE: any line of 1, 2, 3 or 5 broken - whatever the result. A loss that followed every line is "valid setup, market disagreed".
- PROCESS MISTAKE: something wrong around the trade (late call, a test run before its kill line was pushed, more than 3 variants, a missed gate, a wrong number pushed).
- Score: 59 funded trades, 59 valid, 0 trade mistakes; 10 process mistakes.

## 8. How I work
One live day at a time; the result is tagged first. One honest test earns the next day: hypothesis, worked case and kill lines pushed before the run,
max 3 variants, costs on, everything counted in STATE.md (89 ideas, 250 variants, no trading idea adopted). A survivor earns a paper line, never a rule change.
Closed, so that I do not reopen it on a bad day: entry filters from chart reading, entry time and price, the lookback, every exit except noon,
second setups, day filters. The list with results is in the long version, section 7.
