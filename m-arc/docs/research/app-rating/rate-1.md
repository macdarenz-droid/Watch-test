# Gym and strength app competitor scan (Android focus, as of 2026-10-01)

**Method:** I read the live Google Play pages (US store, fetched 2026-10-01), the App Store pages for US, PH and AU prices, the newest US App Store reviews (RSS feed), the official help pages, and a few market sources. Anything not checked against a primary source is marked **[unverified]**.

**Caveats:** Prices come from Apple's in-app purchase lists. Google Play does not show per-plan prices, so Android prices may differ. The Play reviews shown are Google's "most relevant" picks, which lean positive, so the complaints below come mostly from the newest App Store reviews.

## Top complaints (newest reviews)

- **Hevy:** heavy pushing to upgrade; "Strava for lifting" bloat and too many taps; a user who trains by heart rate says the app ignores it; updates that break basics; wants a 5th free routine (US App Store, Sept 2026) [R-hevy]. Play: a bug in the weight-entry date picker [P1].
- **Strong:** no syncing with other apps; Apple Watch problems; paywall on templates; poor search; small library; exercise icons drawn almost all as men (Feb–Mar 2026) [R-strong]. Play: on Android you can't duplicate a single exercise, and replacing an exercise wipes its notes [P2].
- **Fitbod:** price ("$16 a month"); no free use; workouts change too much from week to week; never asks about injuries; calorie numbers wrong; billing and cancelling trouble; daily rate-the-app prompts. 23 of the newest 100 reviews are 1–2 stars (Sept 2026) [R-fitbod].
- **JEFIT:** pop-ups and upgrade prompts; "all this AI stuff has ruined the app"; a timer bar from a recent update blocks swiping; $70/yr felt not worth it. 32 of the newest 100 reviews are 1–2 stars (May–Jul 2026) [R-jefit].
- **Alpha Progression:** charged when the trial ended; setup is hard (2024–25 reviews, so older) [R-alpha]. Play: the workout generator doesn't fit some people's goals [P5].
- **Boostcamp:** features keep moving behind the paywall (swapping an exercise needs Pro); pop-ups (2024–25 reviews) [R-boost]. Play, Jul 2026: on Android the app reloads after you switch apps for a minute [P6].
- **Future:** buggy Android app; the watch disconnects; the "hide calories" setting doesn't work [P7].

## Market signals: Philippines and Australia

- **Philippines:** 86.53% of phones run Android and 13.47% iOS (Sept 2026) [SC-PH]. The AI-planning apps cost about ₱650–900 a month on iOS, while Hevy costs ₱155 [A1p][A3p][A4p]. Market size figures: Statista puts digital fitness and wellbeing at €335.8M for 2024 **[unverified, search snippet]** [ST-PH]; Ken Research says $420M in 2025 **[secondary]** [KR].
- **Australia:** 62.59% iOS and 37.4% Android (Sept 2026) [SC-AU]. The top 5 health and fitness apps by revenue in Q4 2025 were MyFitnessPal, Strava, Flo, Runna and Calm. **No gym logger made the top 5** [STW]. Google's Gemini coach reached Australia on 2026-02-10 [GB]. IMARC says $70.1M in 2025 **[secondary]** [IM].
- **For an Android app:** Ladder has no Android app, Gymshark pulled its app from Play, and Future's Android app has only 10K+ downloads. The Philippines is an Android market with little competition from these premium coaching apps.

## Table stakes in 2026 (most of the leaders list these)

- Fast set logging that shows last session's numbers
- Rest timer, supersets, and set tags (warm-up, drop, failure)
- Charts for 1RM, records (PRs) and volume
- Plate and warm-up calculators
- Unlimited routines, plus custom exercises
- An exercise library with video or animation
- Muscle-group charts
- A Wear OS app with heart rate
- Health Connect sync
- CSV export and cloud backup

Sources: [P1]–[P6], [AP].

## What makes people choose an app, and what makes them pay

1. **Free and simple wins the ratings.** Hevy is the cheapest (from $23.99/yr) and has a generous free tier: 4.9 stars and 5M+ downloads. The pricey AI-first apps (Fitbod, JEFIT) sit at 4.4–4.5 with many 1–2 star reviews about billing and pop-ups [P1][P3][P4][R].
2. **People pay for:**
   - more routines than the free cap (Hevy, Strong) [H1][A2];
   - automatic progression and generated plans (Alpha's paywall, Hevy Trainer, JEFIT Elite, Fitbod) [AP][H2][P4];
   - named-coach programs (Boostcamp) [P6];
   - a human coach (Future at $199/mo, Ladder) [P7][L].
3. **AI is mostly plan generation, not chat.** None of the six logging apps lists a chat coach in its Play description. Hevy sends your data out to ChatGPT or Claude instead [P1]. The only big chat coach (Google's) costs $9.99/mo, needs Google's own wearable, and is not in the Philippines yet [AA][GB].

## Gaps a small solo-built app could own

1. **A free coach chat that knows your own log, on Android.** Nobody here offers one; the nearest is Google's coach, which is paid, needs Google hardware, and isn't in the Philippines. **Risks:** AI costs per message, and health and injury advice; it needs firm safety limits.
2. **Free progression targets plus a recovery model.** Alpha puts progression targets behind Pro, Fitbod's recovery heat map sits behind $15.99/mo, and Hevy has no recovery model [AP][F1][H1].
3. **Using heart rate for readiness, not just showing it.** A Hevy reviewer complains the app ignores heart rate, and Fitbod users say its calorie numbers are wrong [R]. Watch heart rate plus Health Connect, fed into the readiness score, would stand out.
4. **No upgrade prompts, stable plans, and asking about injuries.** These are the top complaints about Hevy, JEFIT, Boostcamp and Fitbod [R].
5. **Illustrated how-to sheets.** Every competitor uses video. My reasoning, not sourced: drawings load fast and work offline, which matters on Philippine mobile data. Drawings that show both men and women also answer the Strong complaint [R-strong].
6. **Reliability on Android.** Boostcamp reloads after app switches, Strong's Android version is missing features, and Future's app is buggy [P6][P2][P7]. This is a low bar to beat.

**Threats:**
- Hevy keeps adding features at a very low price, including Hevy Trainer [H2].
- Google bundles its coach with Health Premium [AA].
- Samsung Health is adding AI workout routines on Galaxy Watch **[unverified date]** [SM].
