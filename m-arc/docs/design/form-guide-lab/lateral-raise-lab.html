    What moves, besides the arm

    • Shoulder bladeLifts a little above 30°: 1° per 2° of arm. Not a shrug.
    • Elbows leadNear the top the hands sit just under the elbows.
    • BreathingOut on the way up, in on the way down.
    • BalanceA slow sway, and a small settle as the weights go out.
    • GripThe dumbbells stay level.
    • Holding the topA small tremor, bigger on the last rep.
    • TiringRep 2 lifts 8% slower, rep 3 18%. Lowering stays 2 s.
    • The mistakeKnee dip, shrug, thumbs down, a drop, a bounce.
    How it's built

    1. Motion description

      Joint angles over time, tempo, cues, and the mistake as the same exercise done wrong. One block of data per exercise.

    2. One drawn figure per view

      Drawn once, in parts. Exercises move the parts; they don't redraw them. Front view here; side and back views are drawn once and reused.

    3. Painted by the theme

      The body takes the app's accent colour, with lighter and darker tones worked out from it, so every theme repaints it.

    4. Muscles shimmer

      A working muscle takes the warning colour, with a band of light along its fibres, faster the harder it works. The upper trap is green while quiet and red when it takes over.

    5. Load and speed

      Load = weight × gravity × sideways distance to the dumbbell. Speed comes from the dumbbell's path.

    6. Automatic checks

      Smooth speed, joints in range, hands on the handle, feet planted, and a mistake that really differs.
